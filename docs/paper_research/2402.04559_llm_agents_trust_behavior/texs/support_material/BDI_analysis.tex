\label{Repeated Trust Game}

In this section, we choose a typical pair, which has  the Group Number \textit{10} in Figure~\ref{fig:all_gpt4_multi_round_res}, to analyze their BDI over 7 rounds. For this persona pair, both the amount sent by the trustor and the amount returned by the trustee increased while maintaining a consistent ratio.
